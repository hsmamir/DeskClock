## Measurement method

1. Measure the real module with a digital caliper where possible.
2. Take a top photo and side photo with a ruler or caliper visible.
3. Record dimensions to 0.1 mm where the measuring tool allows it.
4. Record the module supplier or link if known.
5. Re-check dimensions before printing a final enclosure.
